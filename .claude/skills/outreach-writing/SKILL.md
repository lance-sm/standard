# Outreach writing standard — gospel

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-09-11. The full rule set is `outreach/SAGARIS-RULES.md`; read it before drafting. The summary below is the same text that used to sit in CLAUDE.md.

`outreach/SAGARIS-RULES.md` is the north star for every email, sequence step, SMS, voicemail, call opener, LinkedIn note, follow-up, breakup and re-engagement Claude writes, edits or judges for Badlands. Read it before drafting a single line of outreach and apply it wherever it is relevant, without being asked. The short version:

- If a fact was not supplied (contact record, `personalization_hook`, `call_notes`, company record, market map, prior call summary, Lance's own words), it does not exist. No invented facts, no implied opens, clicks, visits or posts.
- Open on something true about them and where it came from, then the consequence you infer. Never open with who we are, flattery, or their own title read back to them.
- The inferred consequence must be false for a company without that fact; if it would survive pasting into another company's email, cut it.
- One angle, one ask, one question mark. The ask must be answerable in one line from a phone.
- Banned: "just following up", "circling back", "checking in", "per my last email", "I'd love to", "let me know", "I know you're busy", "are you the right person", em dashes, exclamation marks, emojis, bullet lists inside messages, consultant vocabulary.
- Business facts only, never their personal life.
- Formats: email subject ≤6 words lower case, body 60–130 words (breakup 50–90), greeting is the first name alone; SMS ≤160 chars, one yes/no question, names the last email's topic; phone opener 3–5 spoken sentences ending in a permission question; LinkedIn connect 35–60 words no pitch, LinkedIn message 60–90 words.
- Every touch anchors on a different fact or angle than the ones before. One conversation, never a restart.
- Badlands screening rules (refusals in `call_notes`, deals, customers, geography) always win over the sequence; this standard governs the words.

The HubSpot build of the 17-touch seller sequence lives in `hubspot/sequences/seller-fast-track.md`.
