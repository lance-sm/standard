# The email that carries the map

Modeled on the cold email Lance flagged 7/29/26 (the "Origami / Apollo comparison" one that
went around X). What makes that email work is worth stating, because every line here is
doing the same job:

1. **Subject is a document name, not a pitch.** "Origami / Apollo comparison" reads like
   something already in progress.
2. **The sender did a thing.** Not a claim, not a value prop. He ran a test. You mapped a state.
3. **A flat result, one line.** No adjectives.
4. **The sting is an absence, and it is specific.** "Origami was nowhere in the results."
   Verifiable, and it stings precisely because it is checkable.
5. **Reframe as solvable, with proof.** Named logos, no case study attached.
6. **One question, and the artifact is the answer.** "Want me to take you through the audit?"
7. **Offer it, do not attach it.** The reply IS the win. An attachment gets read and filed.
   A question gets answered, and now you are in a conversation.

One idea per line. Nothing wraps. It reads in six seconds on a phone.

## TOUCH 1 - offer the map (send this one)

### Version A - Sellers / buy-box owners

**Subject, pick one:**
- `Connecticut independents - map`
- `[[Company]] / Connecticut security map`
- `mapped the CT independents`

**Body:**

[[First Name]], I spent last week mapping every independent security, locksmith and fire
shop still standing in Connecticut.

Eighty left.

Twenty-one have gone into national and PE-backed rollups since 2015. Six of those went to
the same buyer.

[[Company]] is on the standing list. [[Custom1]]

For context on why I care: I lead development at Badlands. We bought Webster Lock in the
Bronx, one of the largest family-owned locksmiths in the country, and we bought it to keep
it. Not a PE roll-up, no three-year fund clock.

Want me to send you the map? Mostly I want to know whether I got your details right.

Lance

> `[[Custom1]]` = one true, specific detail. Founding year, a generation, a niche they own.
> Delete the line rather than force it.

### Version B - Industry Contacts / OEMs / reps / association people

**Subject, pick one:**
- `Connecticut dealers - who did I miss?`
- `mapped the CT independents`
- `CT security map`

**Body:**

[[First Name]], I mapped every independent security, locksmith and fire company still
standing in Connecticut.

Eighty left.

Twenty-one have been absorbed since 2015, six of them by Pye-Barker. Some of those were
almost certainly your dealers.

The eighty that are left are the interesting part. You are selling into them and I am trying
to get to know them, which makes this a pretty natural place to compare notes.

I lead development at Badlands - we own Webster Lock in the Bronx and we are building the
leading security portfolio in the Northeast, thoughtfully rather than quickly.

Want me to send it over? I would rather hear who I missed than keep passing around a map
with holes in it.

Lance

## TOUCH 2 - deliver it (only after they say yes)

**Subject:** reply in the same thread.

[[First Name]], here it is. Page one is where everyone sits, page two is the list.

The one thing I would ask: if a name is wrong, missing, or already sold, tell me. Every
correction goes straight into the next version.

[[If Seller]] And whenever the question of what is next crosses your mind, we are the rare
buyer where the name stays, the people stay, and you can take a clean exit or roll some
equity and grow it with us. No pressure, and no agenda today.

If you are in the city, are you around for a coffee in the next couple of weeks? Zoom works
too if that is easier.

Lance

> Attach the **external** cut only. The internal cut has succession flags on the recipient,
> which is a bad look and an unforced error.

## Working rules

- **Swap the numbers per state.** Every figure above is on page 1 of that state's map. The
  email and the attachment must agree, so if the state count changes, change both.
- **CT figures verified 7/29/26:** 21 absorbed 2015-2026, six to Pye-Barker, 80 still
  standing. Four of the 21 could not be dated and print without a year.
- **Never open with the ask.** The map is the reason for the note. Selling is what happens
  three emails later, if at all.
- **One idea per line.** If a line wraps twice on a phone, cut it.
- Hyphens, never em dashes. Sign "Lance."
