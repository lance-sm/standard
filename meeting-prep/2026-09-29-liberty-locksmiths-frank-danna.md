## 7. Watch-outs

- **Automotive mix.** 2,800+ Google reviews is a consumer lead-gen business, not a commercial door portfolio. Auto key work is transactional, price-competitive and does not multiple like contracted commercial service. The commercial / facilities / property-management lines are the part that fits the platform — size them early.
